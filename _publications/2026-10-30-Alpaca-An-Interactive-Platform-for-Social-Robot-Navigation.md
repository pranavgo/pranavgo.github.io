Alpaca is an open, full-stack mobile robot platform for benchmarking social robot navigation in the field. Built from off-the-shelf components, it combines humanlike height and walking-pace mobility with 3D sensing and an expressive face, microphone, and speaker. A modular ROS 2 stack integrates pedestrian tracking, human motion prediction, and navigation with conversational interaction. We demonstrate its capabilities through human motion prediction benchmarking in an atrium and interactive guidance in constrained spaces.

The paper is **under review at HRI 2027**.
