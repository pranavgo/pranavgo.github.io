SocRATES is an LLM-powered ROS2/Gazebo pipeline that automatically generates diverse, realistic test scenarios for social navigation algorithms and surfaces failure cases across varied human behaviors and contexts. Code: [github.com/clear-nus/SocRATES](https://github.com/clear-nus/SocRATES).
