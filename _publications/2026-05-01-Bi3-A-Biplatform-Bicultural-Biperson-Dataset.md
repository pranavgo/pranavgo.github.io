A dataset of close social robot navigation encounters between two humans and a robot, collected across two robot platforms and two sites (USA and France) with 74 participants. Bi3 provides 10.5 hours of multimodal human/robot motion tracks, RGB video, and user impressions, offering a benchmark for human motion prediction and robot control policies in densely occupied spaces. Code and data: [github.com/fluentrobotics/bi3dataset](https://github.com/fluentrobotics/bi3dataset).
