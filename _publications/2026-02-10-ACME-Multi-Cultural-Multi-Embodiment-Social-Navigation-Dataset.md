A large-scale social navigation dataset collected across 8 sites in 5 countries with 7 robot embodiments, comprising 29.35 hours of onboard robot data and 43.5 hours of overhead pedestrian tracking, built to capture how culture, geography, and embodiment shape appropriate social navigation behavior.
