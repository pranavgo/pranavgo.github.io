 CLeAR lab in National University of Singapore seeks to improve people’s lives through intelligent robotics. The central focus of the lab is on developing physical and social skills for robots.
 
**Role**:
- Implemented real-world demonstrations of [LTLDoG: Satisfying Temporally-Extended Symbolic Constraints for Safe Diffusion-Based Planning](/publication/2024-01-01-LTLDoG-Satisfying-Temporally-Extended-Symbolic-Constraints-for-Safe-Diffusion-Based-Planning). Using LTLDoG as a global planner, we conducted navigation experiments with a Unitree Go2 robot. The work was published in IEEE Robotics and Automation Letters (RA-L) in 2024.
  
- Developed a ROS 2/Gazebo simulation tool that uses LLMs to generate social navigation scenarios with varied human behaviors and contexts for benchmarking navigation algorithms and identifying failure cases. The initial paper, [Towards Automated Scenario Testing of Social Navigation Algorithms](/publication/2024-01-01-Towards-Automated-Scenario-Testing-of-SOcial-Navigation-Algorithims), was presented at the RSS 2024 workshop on Unsolved Problems in Social Navigation. The expanded journal paper, [SocRATES: Language-Driven Scenario Generation for Evaluating Social Navigation Algorithms](/publication/2026-01-20-SocRATES-Towards-Automated-Scenario-based-Testing-RA-L), was published in IEEE Robotics and Automation Letters (RA-L) in 2026.
